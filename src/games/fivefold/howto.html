{game} is a dice game for 2 to 4 players. Everyone fills in their own 13-box scorecard; the highest total wins.

Your turn

  • Roll the five dice. You may roll up to three times in a turn.
  • After the first roll, tap any dice you want to hold (they rise and show HELD), then roll again: only the dice you did not hold are rolled.
  • After any roll, choose one empty box on your scorecard. Your possible score for each box is shown, but nothing is recorded until you choose. Then the next player rolls.
  • Every box must be filled once. If your dice do not fit a box you still want to fill, you can score 0 there; you will be asked to confirm.

The boxes

  • Ones to Sixes: add up only the dice showing that number.
  • Upper bonus: 35 extra points if your Ones to Sixes add up to 63 or more (for example, three of each number).
  • Three of a Kind: at least three dice the same; score the total of all five dice.
  • Four of a Kind: at least four the same; score the total of all five.
  • Full House: three of one number and two of another: 25.
  • Small Straight: four numbers in a row, such as 2-3-4-5: 30.
  • Large Straight: five numbers in a row: 40.
  • Five of a Kind: all five dice the same: 50.
  • Chance: any dice; score their total.

Simplified rules

This version deliberately leaves out two rules found in some five-dice games: there are no extra bonuses for rolling Five of a Kind again, and no "joker" rules. Five of the same number scores 0 as a Full House or a straight.

The end

After 13 rounds every box is full. Totals include the upper bonus (listed at the end as an adjustment). The highest total wins; equal top totals tie.

The dice are rolled by the game server, never on your device, and every roll is saved, so a reload or a slow connection never changes your dice.
