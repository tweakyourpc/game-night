Four in a Row is for two players. Take turns dropping one disc into the upright grid of 7 columns and 6 rows. A disc falls to the lowest empty space in its column.

Winning

  • Make a line of four or more of your discs: across, up and down, or diagonally. The winning line is highlighted.
  • If the grid fills up with no line of four, the game is a draw.

Dropping a disc

  • Mouse: point at a column to see where your disc would land, then click.
  • Touch: tap a column to see where it would land, then tap the same column again to drop.
  • Keyboard: use the left and right arrow keys to choose a column, then press Enter or Space.

Your disc falls once the move is accepted. Each disc shows its player's symbol as well as their colour.

Table option

Who goes first: the first seat (usually the host), or decided at random when the game starts.
